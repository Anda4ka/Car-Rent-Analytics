Privacy Policy

Car Rent Analytics is an internal reporting tool. It reads advertising statistics via official APIs (Google Ads, Meta Marketing API, TikTok Marketing API, GA4 Data API, Search Console API) and stores them in a private database belonging to the car rental business that authorized access.

Data is used solely for internal dashboards and performance analysis. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for any other purpose.

Access tokens are stored securely on the business's own server and are never exposed publicly.
